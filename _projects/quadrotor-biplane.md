Contributed to the design and analysis of controllers for a quadrotor biplane — a tailsitter-style vehicle that transitions between hover and fixed-wing forward flight — that explicitly account for aerodynamic effects across the full flight envelope: hover, transition, and forward flight. This work was published as "Aerodynamics-Aware Design and Analysis of Controllers for Tailsitter Vehicles" in the 2024 IEEE/ASME Transactions on Mechatronics.
